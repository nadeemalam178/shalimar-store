### 🚀 What's New in Version 2.0.0:

#### 🛍️ Customer Experience & Design
- **Persistent Shopping Cart:** Items added to your cart remain safe in memory and local storage across app restarts until you complete your order on WhatsApp or clear the cart.
- **Animated Splash Screen:** Smooth fade-in, scale, and graceful fade-out transition showcasing the circular Shalimar badge and tagline.
- **Pinned Rounded Search Bar:** Search bar remains permanently frozen/pinned at the top of the store while scrolling through the catalog.
- **Dock Clutter Fix:** The bottom navigation dock and floating cart pill automatically hide while typing in search or forms.
- **Strictly Rounded Design Language:** Unified 20–30px radiuses across all cards, buttons, dialogs, and pill tags.
- **Store Heritage & Shop Photos:** Tap the home banner to view the real storefront photo of **Md. Jamil General Store (Shalimar Store)**, with verified store history, timings, and direct map directions.
- **Relocated UPI Payment Card:** Positioned conveniently right below the "Pay Now with UPI App" button.

#### 🔐 Admin Control & Inventory Management
- **Direct Camera & Gallery Photo Upload:** Snap live photos of products using the camera or select from device files.
- **Dynamic Category Management:** Admin can create and manage new store categories/departments on the fly, instantly reflected for all users.
- **Out-of-Stock Breakdown Dashboard:** Interactive management card showing unavailable items grouped by category with 1-tap instant Restock.
- **Category & Stock Filters:** Filter the admin table by category and availability (All / In Stock / Out of Stock).
- **Security Hardening:** Confidential default login IDs and passwords are removed from all UI fields, placeholders, and hint texts; verified via SHA-256.

#### 📡 Backend-Managed GitHub Releases OTA Update
- Whenever a new release is pushed to GitHub, users running the app automatically receive an in-app banner showing the release changelog and 1-tap APK update download button.
